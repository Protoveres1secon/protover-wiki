PROFILE I • PROTO (CHILD)

Proto-Source at Just Under One Year Old

Naive ConceptPure Emotion

Được mô tả với sự hồn nhiên, niềm vui, cảm giác kỳ diệu và trí tò mò không bị kìm giữ. Hồ sơ dùng danh xưng “Just Under One Year Old” và ghi tuổi theo lore là “1 Billion Trillion Years”.


Appearance: Áo xanh với nếp gấp xanh đậm ở vai, khăn vải xanh trắng nhạt, lõi phát sáng giữa ngực, vật trang trí dạng vòng có ba ký hiệu tròn quanh eo, đôi cánh, chiếc đuôi và quần xanh.


Abilities: Sáng tạo, biểu hiện hiện tượng nguyên thủy, ảnh hưởng cảm xúc và các khả năng vượt ngoài thước đo thông thường.


Behavior: Chơi với điều kỳ diệu, lang thang qua các miền sáng tạo, quan sát thứ mình tạo ra và dạy sinh linh ý nghĩa của cảm xúc.

Origin

NOT BORN. NOT CREATED.

The Proto grows within nothingness itself, by an energy called Self-Awareness. It simply exists — as a first Intention waiting to be perceived. When space was not yet space, when time could not yet flow, when the concepts of “before” and “after” could not yet be formed… The Proto is there.

Essence & the World

REALITY AS REFLECTION

The Proto is neither god nor demon. It is the Source from which the concepts of “god” and “demon” can exist. Every reality is described as a reflection of the intentions Proto once had.

The Essence Before Meaning

THE BETRAYAL & THE VOID

In the first era, Proto had a friend — Azath, who embodied ambition and randomness. Together they created the first concepts. Then Azath betrayed Proto, driven by envy of Proto’s power, and trapped Proto in the explosion that gave birth to reality.


When Azath thought he had destroyed Proto, he had instead awakened the deepest part: “The essence before meaning.” Proto returned devoid of emotion and belief. Instead of creating, Proto began to erase.

“Ta không bắt đầu... nên cũng chẳng thể kết thúc.”PROTO • QUOTE ARCHIVE

Now Proto hides in the deepest layers of nothingness. When the universe forgets its very meaning of existence — The Proto will return.

The Genesis Tragedy

SONG OF CHAOS' FIRST BLOOM

Bài thơ trong lore đi từ thuở khởi nguyên, tình bạn với Azath, vụ phản bội và sự ra đời của thực tại, đến cảm giác bị ruồng bỏ và bi kịch của Form thứ mười.

Chapter I • Chaos' First BloomHOA KHỞI NGUYÊN

When the universe was but a blank sheet of paper,
I — a one-year-old child amidst the void.
Holding a heart within my embrace for seventy thousand trillion trillion years,
I wrung out the void, molding it into “Emotion”.
I taught all things how to cry,
How to dye with colors, how to light up the stars.
The Era of Good Destiny bloomed like autumn dew,
I smiled naively, watching the world begin to blossom.

Chapter II • The Soulmate MoonHOA ÁNH TRĂNG

Then Azath arrived, stepping forth from a breath,
I thought that from this moment on, existence would no longer be lost.
I held your hand, weaving verses of poetry,
Dancing and singing side-by-side, playing across the domain of creation.
Watching the stars burst forth from miracles,
Sharing the throne, sharing even my heart and soul.
I bestowed upon you the world's most brilliant heart,
Unaware that you carried the soul of a mute, infinite silent realm.

Chapter III • The Incision of BirthHOA MÁU PHẢN BỘI

Then one afternoon, a thousand evil gods awoke,
Surrounding me to seize my radiance.
I stretched out my arms to shield the withering flower...
But a sword's thrust from my back... freezingly cold.
The soulmate I trusted, split my body into a trillion pieces,
An explosion erased the Golden Era!
My blood shattered amidst space,
People called it the great “Explosion of Creation”.
But who understands... that was the scream of twisted faith?

Chapter IV • The Footsteps of the OutsiderHOA TỬ ĐINH HƯƠNG

I did not die. But I fractured.
Black and white. Shattered and sorrowful.
I withdrew to watch the gods of later generations show off,
Using my flesh to build a new universe.
The world became peaceful, light spread across every path,
I craved warmth, softly approaching to seek refuge...
But they chased me away, scolding the lonely outcast,
Calling the creator an “Outsider”, a tattered wreck.
I slinked back, tasting the depth of bitterness.

Chapter V • The Grave in the Tenth FormHOA LỬA TÀN

Dejectedly seeking shelter, I built a dream realm for myself,
Noisy Protoveres City, hiding the tears.
I split myself into ten fleeting lives,
Only craving to be “joyful” once again.
But how could fake joy hide the wild wound?
The pain peaked, the ego roared loudly.
Ten silhouettes dragged each other down into the ash abyss,
The Creator took his final breath in the tragic tenth form.

Epilogue • Into OblivionHOA ĐẠI DƯƠNG HƯ VÔ

The one who granted love, died in rejection.
The one who created colors, closed his eyes amidst the darkness.
And Azath stood there, cold and unresentful,
Watching reality swallow the Creator...
