Privacy Policy — Protoveres Wiki

Last updated: September 6, 2026

Protoveres Wiki is a fan-created community and wiki website. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use accounts, social features, messaging, notifications, and other features on the website.

1. Information we may process

  • Account information: information associated with your sign-in method, such as your email address, display name, and account identifier (Firebase UID).
  • Profile information: information you choose to add to your profile, such as username, country, age, avatar, and cover image.
  • Community content: posts, reactions, friend requests, and other content you choose to submit.
  • Messages and shared media: messages, images, GIFs, and videos exchanged through the private 1-to-1 chat system.
  • Notifications: information needed to show account, friend, post, and chat notifications.

2. Sign-in providers

Protoveres Wiki supports Email/Password, Google, and Facebook sign-in through Firebase Authentication. When you use a third-party sign-in provider, that provider may provide basic account information to Firebase according to the permissions and policies applicable to that provider.

3. How information is used

Information is used to provide and operate the website, authenticate accounts, display profiles, connect friends, deliver notifications, provide private chat, display posts and reactions, maintain site functionality, and protect the service from misuse.

4. Data storage and service providers

Protoveres Wiki uses Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, and may use Firebase Storage for uploaded media. These services process data on behalf of the website according to their own terms and privacy policies.

5. Public and private content

Information you choose to publish publicly, such as public profile information or public posts, may be visible to other members of Protoveres Wiki. Private chat messages are intended to be accessible only to the participants in that conversation, subject to the technical operation and security of the service.

6. Message recall and deletion

The website supports message recall. When a message is recalled, the website can mark the message as revoked so that the chat displays that it has been recalled.

7. Your choices

You can choose what profile information and community content you provide. You may also request deletion of your account and associated personal data as described on our Delete User Data page.

8. Children's privacy

The website is not intended to knowingly collect unnecessary personal information from children. Users should only provide information they are permitted to share and should avoid posting sensitive personal information in public areas of the site.

9. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the website's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, please use the contact method provided on the Delete User Data page.
