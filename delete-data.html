Delete User Data — Protoveres Wiki

To request deletion of your Protoveres Wiki account or associated personal data, contact us at nguyentuankietproto@gmail.com.

1. How to request deletion

Send a deletion request from the email associated with your account when possible. Include your Protoveres Wiki email address and/or Firebase UID so we can identify the correct account.

2. What can be deleted

A request may cover the user's Protoveres Wiki account and eligible personal data associated with that account, including profile information stored by the website.

3. Public content

If you also want public posts, reactions, or other community content removed, mention that in the request. Public content may be handled separately from account data.

4. What happens after a request

We will review the request and remove or anonymize eligible personal data, subject to technical limitations, security requirements, legal obligations, backups, or records that must be retained for legitimate reasons.

5. Example request

"Please delete my Protoveres Wiki account and associated personal data. My account email is [your email] and my Firebase UID is [your UID]. Please also remove my public posts."
